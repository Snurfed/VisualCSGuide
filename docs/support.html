Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Go to Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

Why isn't my voice being recognized?

Make sure you've granted microphone permission in your device Settings (Settings → CS Micro-lessons → Microphone). Also ensure you're in a quiet environment and speaking clearly.

Can I sync my progress across devices?

Currently, progress is stored locally on your device. iCloud sync may be added in a future update.

How does the spaced repetition work?

Cards you mark as "Got it" will reappear less frequently, while cards marked "Review" will come back sooner. The system adapts to help you focus on concepts you're still learning.

I found an error in the content. How can I report it?

Please email us with the module name, card title, and description of the error. We appreciate your help in improving the content!

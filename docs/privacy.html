Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

CS Micro-lessons ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

Learning Progress

Your study progress (cards completed, mastery levels, streaks) is stored locally on your device using Apple's standard storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Voice Explanations

When you use the "Explain" feature, your voice is processed using Apple's on-device Speech Recognition framework. Your spoken words are transcribed locally. If you have AI feedback enabled, the text transcription may be sent to our AI service provider (Anthropic) to generate educational feedback. No audio recordings are stored or transmitted.

Microphone Access

The App requests microphone access solely for the voice explanation feature. You can use all other features without granting microphone permission.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, phone)
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs
  • We do not serve advertisements
  • We do not sell any data to third parties

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

Data Storage

All your learning data is stored locally on your device. If you delete the App, your progress data is also deleted.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App is educational in nature and suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
